KEY OBSERVATIONS:
=================

1. MAIN THREAD NEVER BLOCKS
   - Always reading audio from subprocess
   - Always detecting silence
   - Always checking for results

2. TRANSCRIPTIONS HAPPEN IN BACKGROUND
   - User can keep speaking
   - Queue accumulates tasks if needed
   - Coalescing prevents backlog

3. TWO TYPES OF TRANSCRIPTIONS
   - Delta (new audio only): Fast, intermediate feedback
   - Full (entire buffer): Slow, high quality, context-aware

4. OUTPUT PROGRESSION
   - Intermediate outputs append (no deletion)
   - Final output replaces all (deletes intermediate chars)

5. BUFFER LIFECYCLE
   - Grows during speech
   - Preserved through intermediate transcriptions
   - Cleared only after final (2.0s) transcription
